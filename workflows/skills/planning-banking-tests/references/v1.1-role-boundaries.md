## 1. Why this document exists

`planning-banking-tests` v1.0.0 ships as a **single-LLM-call monolithic skill** consuming the full BA `output.json` and producing one QA plan envelope. This is dictated by ADR #8 in `COGNITIVE_OS_PROJECT.md` Section 2 ("Single LLM per stage | defer multi-role to v2") and reinforced by §13 non-goal "No multi-agent orchestration — single LLM per stage." Cost analysis on the T2 holdout (`e-commerce-v5`, 16 stories, 5 epics) further showed monolithic is the only configuration that clears the T2 envelope of <$1.50/run at GPT-5.5 Pro pricing.

The research session that produced v1.0.0 nevertheless did substantial decomposition work — partitioning the QA planning task into 10 candidate roles, mapping their inputs to BA-output keys, and proving four of them fire conditionally on the holdout. This document preserves that work so v1.1+ (when ADR #8 is relaxed for workflow sub-graphs at Stage 4c, or when prompt-caching changes the cost arithmetic) does not re-derive role boundaries from scratch. Treat sections 2–7 as **frozen research artifacts**; treat sections 8–10 as the migration brief for v1.1+.

## 2. The 10 candidate roles

| # | Role | Charter (1 sentence) | Class | Conditional predicate |
|---|------|----------------------|-------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Strategy / Test Architect | Set test frame, risk tiers, environments, exit criteria for the initiative. | mandatory | always fires |
| 2 | Per-Story SDET (×N) | Convert each story's acceptance_criteria + banking_grade_concerns into executable test cases. | mandatory | one instance per `stories[*]` |
| 3 | Security Test Lead | Specify authn/authz, PII-redaction, masking, tipping-off tests. | conditional | `len(pii_inventory) > 0` OR any story has `banking_grade_concerns.authn_authz.status == "applies"` |
| 4 | Performance Test Lead | Specify load, soak, race-condition, idempotency-under-concurrency tests. | conditional | `frontmatter.workload_tier in {"T1","T2"}` AND any story has `banking_grade_concerns.idempotency.status == "applies"` |
| 5 | Compliance / Regulatory Test Lead | Map each `regulatory_dependencies[*].code` to evidence-producing test cases. | conditional | `len(regulatory_dependencies) > 0` |
| 6 | Test Data Engineer | Build masked/synthetic fixtures honoring `masking_rule` + `residency_rule` per PII row. | mandatory | always fires when stories exist |
| 7 | Environment Owner | Specify env matrix (browser, region, payment-mock vs real, feature flags). | mandatory | always fires |
| 8 | Sign-off Officer | Assemble go/no-go evidence packet against `dor_checklist` + `governance_gaps`. | mandatory | runs last but always fires |
| 9 | Coverage Auditor | Verify every story / AC / banking-grade concern has at least one test mapped. | mandatory | runs final, merges fragments |
| 10 | Accessibility Test Lead | WCAG 2.1 AA cases for customer-facing surfaces. | conditional + collapsible into SDET | any story has `card.when` mentioning guest/customer surface AND `scope_kind` is customer-facing |

Collapsibility note: Roles 6 (Test Data) and 10 (Accessibility) can be absorbed into Per-Story SDET prompts for low-tier (T3) workloads where budget pressure outweighs separation-of-concerns benefit.

## 4. Per-role JSONPath slices

All paths below were validated against the e-commerce-v5 holdout via `jq` (16 stories, 5 epics, 10 PII fields, 4 regulatory codes).

**Strategy / Test Architect**
- `$.initiative` (title, summary, epic_ids, per_epic_tier[*])
- `$.frontmatter.workload_tier`
- `$.epics[*].{id,title,inferred_tier,success_criteria,stakeholders,scope}`
- `$.governance_gaps[?(@.severity in ['high','critical'])]`

**Per-Story SDET (one instance per story)**
- `$.stories[i].{id,title,card,context,priority,sizing,dependencies}`
- `$.stories[i].acceptance_criteria[*].{scenario_name,scenario_type,given,when,then}`
- `$.stories[i].banking_grade_concerns.{idempotency,reversibility,authn_authz,audit_events,pii_fields,regulatory,tipping_off}`
- `$.stories[i].dor_checklist`
- `$.frontmatter.workload_tier` (read-only)

**Security Test Lead**
- `$.pii_inventory[*].{field,category,treatment,masking_rule,residency_rule,retention_rule,access_audit}`
- `$.stories[?(@.banking_grade_concerns.authn_authz.status=='applies' || @.banking_grade_concerns.pii_fields.status=='applies' || @.banking_grade_concerns.tipping_off.status=='applies')]`
- `$.governance_gaps[?(@.type=='pii_inventory_missing' || @.type=='retention_policy_unstated')]`

**Performance Test Lead**
- `$.frontmatter.workload_tier`
- `$.initiative.per_epic_tier[*]`
- `$.stories[?(@.banking_grade_concerns.idempotency.status=='applies' || @.banking_grade_concerns.reversibility.status=='applies')]`
- `$.stories[*].acceptance_criteria[?(@.scenario_type=='banking_grade_idempotency' || @.scenario_type=='edge_case')]`

**Compliance / Regulatory Test Lead**
- `$.regulatory_dependencies[*].{code,regulator,promisor,revision,due_date,citation_status}`
- `$.stories[?(@.banking_grade_concerns.regulatory.status=='applies')].id`
- `$.governance_gaps[?(@.type=='regulatory_citation_unresolved' || @.type=='legal_absent_on_regulatory' || @.type=='compensating_action_missing')]`
- `$.open_questions[?(@.severity=='blocker')]` (if regulatory)

**Test Data Engineer**
- `$.pii_inventory[*].{field,category,masking_rule,residency_rule}`
- `$.stories[?(@.banking_grade_concerns.pii_fields.status=='applies')].id`

**Environment Owner**
- `$.initiative.scope`, `$.initiative.tier_signals`
- `$.frontmatter.workload_tier`
- `$.assumptions_made` (e.g. mock-payment / mock-shipping flags)
- `$.epics[*].scope`

**Sign-off Officer**
- `$.frontmatter.ba_confidence`
- `$.ba_compliance_checklist`
- `$.governance_gaps[?(@.blocks_tl_handoff==true)]`
- `$.open_questions[?(@.blocks_tl_handoff==true)]`
- `$.assumptions_made[?(@.blocks_tl_handoff==true)]`

**Coverage Auditor**
- `$.stories[*].id` × `$.stories[*].acceptance_criteria[*].scenario_name`
- `$.pii_inventory[*].field`
- `$.regulatory_dependencies[*].code`
- `$.governance_gaps[*].type`

**Accessibility Test Lead**
- `$.stories[?(@.card.when =~ /guest|customer|user/i)]`
- `$.epics[?(@.scope =~ /customer|web|UI/i)]`

## 5. Conditional firing predicates

All predicates are mechanically evaluable from `output.json` without LLM judgment. Test on holdout (`e-commerce-v5`) reports — all four fire:

| Role | Predicate (jq-equivalent) | Holdout result |
|---|---|---|
| Security | `(.pii_inventory \| length) > 0 or (any(.stories[]; .banking_grade_concerns.authn_authz.status=="applies"))` | **TRUE** (10 PII fields; authn_authz applies on multiple stories) |
| Performance | `(.frontmatter.workload_tier \| IN("T1","T2")) and any(.stories[]; .banking_grade_concerns.idempotency.status=="applies")` | **TRUE** (T2; idempotency applies on CART-CHECKOUT stories) |
| Compliance | `(.regulatory_dependencies \| length) > 0` | **TRUE** (4 codes: PDPA-TH-2019, TRC-VAT-7PCT, CCA-TH-LOG-90D, CPA-TH-DISCLOSURE) |
| Accessibility | `any(.stories[]; .card.when \| test("guest\|customer\|user";"i"))` | **TRUE** (16/16 stories — public B2C surface) |

## 7. Cost analysis snapshot (for v1.1+ revisit)

> Costs assume **GPT-5.5 Pro T2 pricing as of 2026-04 ($5 / $30 per 1M prompt/completion tokens)**. Re-measure before any v1.1+ implementation; introduction of prompt caching alone is expected to invert the ranking.

| Configuration | Prompt tokens | Completion tokens | Calls | Total ($) | T2 envelope <$1.50? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monolithic (v1.0.0 ships this)** | 51k | 30k | 1 | **$1.15** | YES |
| Fan-out, sliced inputs (only required JSONPath per role) | ~199k | ~57k | 22 LLM | **$2.72** | NO |
| Fan-out, broadcast (each role sees full output.json) | ~1.17M | ~58k | 22 LLM | **$7.55** | NO |
| Fan-out + prompt-cache (90% read-only hit on broadcast) | ~117k effective | ~58k | 22 LLM | **~$2.30** (projected) | NO (still over) |

Broadcast configuration is the worst at 6.5× monolithic. Sliced fan-out is 2.4× monolithic. None clear T2 at v1.0.0 prices.

## 8. Why v1.0.0 ships monolithic

- **ADR #8** (`COGNITIVE_OS_PROJECT.md` §2): "Single LLM per stage" is a locked decision; multi-role is deferred to v2.
- **Cost envelope**: every fan-out shape exceeds T2's <$1.50/run. Monolithic at $1.15 is the only compliant configuration.
- **Audit-schema incompatibility**: §8 stage audit event schema is **flat** (one record per stage). It has no array field for sub-call records, so fan-out today would either (a) silently lose sub-call telemetry or (b) require schema migration on the compacted 7-year-retention topic — out of scope for v1.0.0.

## 9. What would unlock v1.1+ fan-out

- ADR #8 relaxes for Stage 4c — workflow sub-graph permitted **OR** Stage 4c is migrated to a workflow YAML that decomposes it into sibling stages (each a "single LLM per stage" in the literal sense).
- T2 cost envelope is raised **OR** prompt-caching is integrated end-to-end (projected ~70% drop on broadcast prompt tokens; needs re-measurement at v1.1 prices).
- §8 audit schema gains an array field `sub_stage_records[*]` with the same field set as the parent record **OR** each sub-stage emits its own envelope event into a derived topic (`audit.workflow.substages.planning-banking-tests`).
- A determinism contract is specified for the multi-call case: seed pinning across role calls, **sorted dispatch** (lexicographic by role name) so ordering doesn't perturb hashes, response cache keyed on `(role, input_hash)`.
- Sign-off Officer's merge contract gets a formal JSON schema (currently implicit in the monolithic prompt).
- **Strict normalizer step between sub-agent fragments and the merger.** Empirically validated on test case 001 (`tests/cases/001-ecommerce-multi-epic.input.json`): independent agents producing fragments drift on vocabulary — regulator codes (short form `TRC` vs canonical `TRC_VAT_7PCT`), PII field names (`customer.email` vs bare `email`), compliance tags. Without normalization, 3 of 5 quality assertions failed on the e-commerce holdout even though every fragment individually parsed and validated. After running `scripts/normalize_fragments.py` (12 regulator remaps + 14 PII remaps + 30 compliance-tag remaps across 5 fragments), traceability and regulator-coverage assertions flipped to PASS. Conclusion: v1.1+ fan-out is non-viable without a normalizer that grounds fragment vocabulary against BA's authoritative codes — `regulatory_dependencies[].code` and `pii_inventory[].field`.

## 10. Re-running this research

Checklist for a future engineer when a new brief lands or when assumptions shift:

1. Pull a fresh holdout brief and its `output.json` (must be T2 or higher; T3 doesn't stress conditional roles).
2. Re-run §5 predicates as `jq` one-liners — record which conditional roles fire.
3. Re-measure prompt token counts for each role's sliced input on the new brief.
4. Re-price using current model card (note: re-check $/M prompt and $/M completion separately — they move independently).
5. Diff `output.json` key set against §3 matrix; add columns for any new top-level keys.
6. Diff banking-grade-concern sub-keys against §4 SDET slice (`idempotency`, `reversibility`, `authn_authz`, `audit_events`, `pii_fields`, `regulatory`, `tipping_off`) — flag any new sub-keys.
7. Re-check ADR #8 status in `COGNITIVE_OS_PROJECT.md` §2 (still locked? relaxed?).
8. Re-check §8 stage audit event schema — does `sub_stage_records[*]` exist yet?
9. Verify prompt-cache availability + hit-rate assumptions against current vendor docs.
10. If all five gates in §9 are green, lift this document from `reference-only` to `design-input` and proceed with workflow YAML decomposition.
